Method

How the work gets done

A six-stage operating model for SEO, AEO, and GEO programs, from intent discovery to measurement and reporting.

//01

Intent and question discovery

Map queries, AI Overview exposure, and page ownership before writing. Know which questions the page must answer and which surface should quote it.

OutputQuery and question map
//02

Brand and entity intelligence

Positioning, audiences, personas, and voice inputs that keep content consistent with the business and recognizable to answer engines.

OutputEntity and voice brief
//03

Answer-first content architecture

Definitional intros, question-led sections, sourced claims, and FAQ structure. The opening paragraph answers the query before the page expands.

OutputAnswer-first page architecture
//04

Technical extractability

Schema, semantic HTML, internal links, indexation, and fast pages. Content cannot be cited if it cannot be parsed and reached.

OutputSchema and technical spec
//05

AI-assisted production with human QA

Content calendars, briefs, and prompt libraries, with claim-level human review of sources, tone, and accuracy before publication.

OutputReviewed content batch
//06

Measurement and reporting

Semrush AI Visibility, GSC Generative AI Features, citation logs, GA4, and CRM context, reported with dates and attribution.

OutputMonthly performance report
