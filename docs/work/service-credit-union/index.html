How we measured Service Credit Union's visibility in AI answers

I audited eight priority pages and used Semrush and Google Search Console to see which pages AI tools cited and how often the site appeared in Google's AI features.

Client
Service Credit Union
servicecu.org · New Hampshire
My role
AI visibility strategy and page audits with SEO leads
Period
Jul 12 – Sep 7, 2026 (Semrush)
August 2026 (GSC)
Data
Semrush AI Visibility
GSC Generative AI Features
01 · Objective

Help AI tools cite the right pages

We wanted Service Credit Union's product, membership, and educational pages to give AI Overviews and conversational tools clear answers they could cite. We also wanted more people who were ready to compare products or apply to click through from regular search. We tracked citations and search click-through rate (CTR) separately so we could see how each was moving.

02 · Insight

Google AI Overviews led in Semrush

In the Semrush snapshot, Google AI Overviews led ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI Mode. Citations edged up from 888 to 892, while the number of cited pages rose from 363 to 382. Most of the gains came from explainers, membership pages, and product pages. The two reporting windows overlap, so these figures show the direction of change.

Reported impressions in Google's AI features rose 7.1%, from 169,000 in July to 180,938 in August. The July figure came from the July 2026 performance deck. The August figure came from the Google Search Console (GSC) Generative AI Features export. The reports measure visibility. Clicks, calls, and leads from AI features would need separate attribution.

03 · Strategy

Put the answer where readers can find it

  • Answer the main question or define the term in the opening paragraph.
  • Use H2s and H3s for the follow-up questions readers are likely to ask.
  • Name authoritative sources so readers can check important facts.
  • Add useful FAQ content and use structured data where it fits the page.
  • Measure citations by URL so it's clear which pages AI tools use as sources.
04 · Execution

Audit the pages and compare the reports

I helped shape the strategy with SEO leads, directors, and account managers. I then audited eight priority pages, checking metadata, H1 through H4 headings, copy, tables, FAQs, JSON-LD, canonicals, robots directives, and llms.txt. I recommended clearer openings, headings based on reader questions, stronger sourcing, and fixes for gaps in FAQPage markup.

I used Semrush Cited Pages to see which URLs AI tools cited and GSC Generative AI Features to see where Google recorded impressions.

The auto loan URLs show why we checked page by page. In Semrush, the new URL went from 8 to 26 cited prompts while the old URL fell from 65 to 49. Across both URLs, the combined count only moved from 73 to 75. The membership page and credit card APR guide each gained cited prompts on the same URL.
