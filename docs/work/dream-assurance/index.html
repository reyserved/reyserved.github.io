01 · Objective

Build a repeatable system, not a one-off campaign

Dream Assurance is an independent insurance agency in Overland Park, Kansas. My responsibility was the whole search program: technical audits, content strategy, schema, internal linking, local SEO, conversion tracking, and reporting.

The GEO question was practical: where are AI Overviews already present in the agency's query set, and which pages are closest to being citable?

02 · Insight

AI Overviews touched most of the tracked query set

In the February 2026 Semrush snapshot, 561 of 685 tracked queries (81.9%) triggered AI Overviews. Only 107 of those AI Overview terms also ranked in the top 10.

Coverage was concentrated in definitional and cost-content pages: indemnity-insurance with 99 AI Overview queries, average-semi-insurance-cost with 79, professional-indemnity-insurance with 43, employment-practices-liability-insurance with 32, and media-liability-insurance with 23.

03 · Strategy

Prioritize by business value and AI citation potential

The operating model joined four layers:

  • Intent and question discovery: map queries, AI Overview exposure, and page ownership.
  • Answer-first content: define the query in the opening paragraph, then answer follow-ups in question-led sections with sources.
  • Technical extractability: schema, internal linking, indexation, and clean page structure.
  • AI-assisted production with human QA: content calendars and briefs grounded in brand intelligence and audience personas.
04 · Execution

Technical, content, and measurement work in one system

Execution included technical audits, JSON-LD schema graphs and validation, FAQ and schema parity checks, internal-link and jump-link audits, a contractor hub with 18 child pages, and GTM/GA4 conversion tracking.

On the content operations side, I built AI supporting content calendars and briefs using brand-intelligence and persona inputs, with human review of claims and sources before publication.

What this proves, and what it does not

What the evidence supports

  • AI Overview exposure is extensive in this insurance query set, so GEO is a planning requirement.
  • A prioritized page list exists, based on AI Overview coverage and current ranking proximity.
  • The program combines technical, content, local, and measurement work under one owner.
  • AI-assisted content production is governed by brand, persona, and source checks.

What it does not support

  • This is an operating-system and opportunity-mapping case, not a traffic-growth case.
  • The February 2026 snapshot is a point-in-time export and is not presented as improvement.
  • No clean before-and-after AI citation trend is available for Dream Assurance.
  • The three-client AI Overview mapping is not full coverage of the Rocket Clicks client roster.
