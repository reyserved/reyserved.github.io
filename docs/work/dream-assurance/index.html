01 · Objective

Migrate and restructure without losing search equity

Dream Assurance is an independent insurance agency in Overland Park, Kansas. Between November 2024 and December 2025, I worked with the CEO on strategy and led the day-to-day execution of the site migration, information architecture, brand intelligence, and answer-first SEO and GEO work for the business. The migration went live in November 2025.

The goal was not just to preserve rankings during the migration. It was to rebuild the site so that service pages and blogs could rank for featured snippets and become a primary source or recommendation in LLM answers.

02 · Insight

AI Overviews touched most of the query set

In the February 2026 Semrush snapshot, 561 of 685 tracked queries (81.9%) triggered AI Overviews. Only 107 of those AI Overview terms also ranked in the top 10.

The Semrush SERP Features Trend shows AI Overview keyword coverage growing from 4 in July 2025 to 21 in December 2025 and 65 in March 2026, with the inflection beginning immediately after the October optimization and November migration. A separate GSC Generative AI features report records 78.3K impressions, with data available only from May 2026 onward.

That made the rebuild a two-surface problem: traditional rankings and answer extraction. The pages with the most AI Overview coverage were indemnity-insurance (99 queries), average-semi-insurance-cost (79), professional-indemnity-insurance (43), employment-practices-liability-insurance (32), and media-liability-insurance (23).

03 · Strategy

Migration, architecture, brand intelligence, and answer-first content

The strategy joined four layers:

  • Migration and architecture: URL mapping, 301 redirects, internal linking, and a cleaner page hierarchy.
  • Brand and entity intelligence: positioning, tone of voice, audience personas, and brand inputs that make the business easier for answer engines to describe accurately.
  • Answer-first content: definitional intros, question-led sections, sourced claims, and FAQ blocks built for featured snippets and LLM extraction.
  • Technical extractability: JSON-LD schema graphs, FAQ parity, indexation checks, and AI-assisted content operations with human QA.
04 · Execution

Migration work plus page-level optimization

Execution included the November 2025 site migration and redirect mapping, architecture and internal-link updates, schema graphs and validation, FAQ and schema parity checks, and answer-first rewrites across service pages and blogs.

I also built the contractor hub with 18 child pages, produced AI supporting content calendars and briefs using brand-intelligence and persona inputs, and maintained GTM/GA4 conversion tracking throughout.
