Rebuilding Dream Assurance's site for search

I led the site migration and SEO work for an independent insurance agency. From December 2025 through February 2026, organic clicks rose 63% and impressions rose 104.8% compared with September through November.

01 · Objective

Protect search visibility during the move

Dream Assurance Group is an independent insurance agency in Overland Park, Kansas. From November 2024 through December 2025, I worked with the CEO on strategy and led the day-to-day work on the migration, site structure, brand research, and SEO. The rebuilt site went live in November 2025.

We wanted to protect existing rankings during the move and make it easier for people to find the right service page or guide. We also wrote those pages to give search engines and AI tools clear passages they could use for featured snippets and answers.

02 · Insight

AI Overviews appeared on most of the tracked queries

In Semrush's February 2026 snapshot, 561 of 685 tracked queries (81.9%) triggered AI Overviews. Of those queries, 107 also had a top-10 ranking for the site. The pages with the most AI Overview exposure covered indemnity insurance (99 queries), average semi insurance cost (79), professional indemnity insurance (43), employment practices liability insurance (32), and media liability insurance (23).

Semrush's SERP Features Trend tracked 4 AI Overview keywords in July 2025, 21 in December, and 65 in March 2026. A separate Google Search Console report recorded 78.3K Generative AI feature impressions, with data available from May 2026 onward. These reports show keyword coverage and impressions. Citation trends would require separate data.

03 · Strategy

Plan the redirects, site structure, and content together

  • Map old URLs to their new destinations with 301 redirects. Make the page hierarchy clearer and update internal links to match.
  • Use audience research, brand positioning, and voice guidelines to explain who the agency serves and what it offers.
  • Open service pages and blog posts with direct answers. Use question headings, cite sources for important claims, and add FAQs where they help readers.
  • Validate JSON-LD, check that FAQ markup matches the visible text, and review indexation. Keep human review in the AI-assisted publishing workflow.
04 · Execution

Launch the migration and update the pages

I handled redirect mapping, page hierarchy, and internal links for the November 2025 migration. I also rewrote service pages and blog posts to answer questions sooner, built and validated JSON-LD schema graphs, and checked that the FAQ markup matched the on-page answers.

I built the contractor insurance hub and its 18 child pages, used audience and brand research to develop supporting content calendars and briefs, and maintained conversion tracking in Google Tag Manager and GA4 throughout the work.

The Search Console comparison shows how the site performed after the migration and optimization work. Several changes went live over that period, so the data doesn't isolate the contribution of any single change.
