About the project

TDE Family Law needed one page for people arriving with different questions about divorce. I used a clear opening message and jump links so visitors could go straight to the issue on their mind.

01

Challenge

Someone looking into an uncontested divorce may have different questions from someone facing a high-asset divorce or considering legal separation. The page needed to match those search intents early, explain the firm’s approach, and keep the consultation CTA easy to find.

The brief
  • Audience

    Atlanta-area visitors looking for divorce or family law help.

  • Goal

    Make it easy to request a confidential consultation or call the firm.

  • Constraint

    Cover several divorce search intents with clear, sensitive language.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Hero with two contact options

    The first screen pairs the firm’s “Fight Smart” message with a phone CTA and a consultation form.

  2. 02

    Jump links by issue

    On-page anchors lead to uncontested divorce, contested divorce, high-asset divorce, LGBTQ+ divorce, and legal separation.

  3. 03

    What clients are facing

    A short section acknowledges the decisions people are weighing before introducing the services.

  4. 04

    Divorce and separation sections

    Each anchored section explains the issue, how the firm approaches it, and what visitors can expect from the team.

  5. 05

    Process and attorneys

    A three-step overview and attorney profiles show who clients may work with and how the process starts.

  6. 06

    FAQ and closing CTA

    Common questions come first, followed by a consultation prompt and a short note about confidentiality.

03

Page architecture

  • Hero and formFirm positioning, phone CTA, and consultation form.
  • Issue jump linksFive on-page links for divorce and separation topics.
  • Trust barExperience, Atlanta-area focus, and client proof.
  • What’s at stakeThe decisions clients face and how the firm can help.
  • Practice areasAnchored sections for divorce and separation.
  • ProcessConsultation, strategy, and action.
  • TeamAttorney profiles.
  • FAQCommon questions about divorce.
  • Closing CTARequest a confidential consultation.
04

Outcome

  • Visitors can jump straight to the issue that brought them to the page.
  • The process overview and attorney profiles give visitors a clearer idea of what happens when they reach out.
  • The consultation CTA appears in the hero, midway down the page, and at the end.
