About the project

Badger Meter offers several ways to measure flow in HVAC systems. The page helps technical buyers narrow down their options by application and installation requirements.

01

Challenge

A chilled water loop, a retrofit, and a system that needs BMS integration each raise different questions. The page needed enough engineering detail for buyers to assess fit, with a quick way to find the section that applied to their system.

The brief
  • Audience

    Facility engineers, HVAC contractors, and building operations teams.

  • Goal

    Get an expert inquiry with enough system detail to route it.

  • Constraint

    Cover multiple meter technologies and installation conditions on one page.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Installation photo and expert CTA

    The hero pairs a real installation photo with a short explanation and a Talk to an Expert CTA above the fold.

  2. 02

    Jump links

    Anchor links take buyers straight to Benefits, Applications, Technologies, or Products.

  3. 03

    What the data helps teams do

    Three cards explain how to get dependable readings, choose a meter that fits the install, and spot problems earlier.

  4. 04

    Application sections

    Chilled water balancing, condenser loop monitoring, and makeup water or glycol detection each get their own section.

  5. 05

    Technology comparison

    Buyers can review inline and clamp-on ultrasonic meters, hydronic energy measurement, and BMS integration in one place.

  6. 06

    Consultation form

    The form asks about the system so the expert has context for the first conversation.

03

Page architecture

  • HeroHVAC metering overview and expert CTA.
  • Jump linksBenefits, applications, technologies, and products.
  • BenefitsWhat HVAC teams can do with reliable flow data.
  • ApplicationsChilled water, condenser water, and makeup water.
  • TechnologiesInline, clamp-on ultrasonic, and hydronic options.
  • Using meter dataHow readings inform HVAC decisions.
  • Solution areasHeating and cooling, water, energy, and wastewater.
  • Key benefitsA quick recap for buyers comparing options.
  • Case studiesExamples of HVAC flow measurement in use.
  • Consultation formRequest help choosing an HVAC metering option.
04

Outcome

  • HVAC teams can compare meter options on the same page.
  • Paid search visitors can reach the expert CTA from the first screen and find engineering details farther down.
  • The application and technology sections also give teams a reference for the HVAC product line.
