About the project

LJP offers waste audits, recycling, zero-landfill programs, waste-to-energy, secure destruction, and program management. The page brings those services together so buyers can see what a full program could look like and ask for a review of their own.

01

Challenge

A buyer might start with a recycling stream, a landfill reduction goal, or a secure destruction project. The page needed to help them find that service and show how it could fit into a broader program. Case studies give buyers examples of how the services work together.

The brief
  • Audience

    Manufacturers, commercial operations teams, and sustainability teams.

  • Goal

    Get consultation requests for a waste program review.

  • Constraint

    Cover six services across several industries and give buyers context for the zero-landfill offering.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Facility photo and two CTAs

    The split hero shows a real facility and gives visitors two ways to continue. They can request a consultation or view case studies.

  2. 02

    Six service cards

    The cards cover waste audits, recycling, zero landfill, waste-to-energy, secure destruction, and LJP 360 program management.

  3. 03

    What the services support

    The Why LJP section explains what the combined services can do for an operations team.

  4. 04

    Industries served

    Cards help visitors find their sector, including manufacturing, distribution, food, and healthcare.

  5. 05

    Case studies

    Program examples show how LJP combines services in practice.

  6. 06

    Closing consultation CTA

    After the case studies and FAQ, visitors can request a review of their own program.

  • Closing CTARequest a waste program review.
04

Outcome

  • Operations teams can start with the service they need and see how it fits into a broader program.
  • The industry cards and case studies give buyers examples to explore before reaching out.
  • The closing CTA gives visitors a way to discuss their own waste program.
