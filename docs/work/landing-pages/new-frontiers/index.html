About the project

New Frontiers offers coaching for students and working professionals. I brought both services onto one page by showing how executive function skills apply in class, early in a career, and at work.

01

Challenge

Planning, prioritizing, getting started, staying organized, and following through matter in school and at work. The page had to explain those skills in everyday terms, speak to four audiences, and show what a free discovery call is for.

The brief
  • Audience

    High school students, college students, early-career professionals, and people looking for support at work.

  • Goal

    Help visitors see where they fit and book a free discovery call.

  • Constraint

    Explain two coaching frameworks and speak to four audiences on one page.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Coaching overview

    A warm photo and a plain-language introduction lead into the free discovery call CTA.

  2. 02

    Skills that carry over

    A short section shows how the same executive function skills apply to school and work.

  3. 03

    RASP and RISE

    Two cards introduce the coaching frameworks and give visitors a quick sense of how each works.

  4. 04

    Coaching by stage

    High school, college, early career, and workplace sections each describe relevant goals and ways to get started.

  5. 05

    Results and testimonials

    Client quotes and examples of coaching outcomes lead into how sessions work.

  6. 06

    How coaching works

    Session details and pricing context help visitors decide what to ask about on the discovery call.

03

Page architecture

  • HeroCoaching overview and discovery call CTA.
  • Shared skillsExecutive function skills behind both services.
  • FrameworksAn introduction to RASP and RISE.
  • Who we supportHigh school, college, early career, and workplace coaching.
  • ResultsExamples of coaching outcomes.
  • TestimonialsClient quotes.
  • How it worksWhat online sessions look like.
  • PricingWhat to know about the cost of coaching.
  • Closing CTABook a free discovery call to talk through your options.
04

Outcome

  • The page explains the connection between academic and career coaching.
  • Students and professionals can find examples relevant to their stage on the same page.
  • The free discovery call gives people a place to ask questions about fit.
