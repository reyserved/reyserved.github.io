About the project

Property owners often start with the problem they can see, like a roof that keeps leaking or cracks they’re worried about. From there, the page helps them find the relevant defect category, learn about the firm’s trial work, and request a case review.

01

Challenge

Someone dealing with a roof leak, window issue, or structural concern may already be juggling repairs and conversations with a contractor or insurer. The page needed to cover six types of defects in plain language and make it easy for visitors arriving from paid search to ask about their situation.

The brief
  • Audience

    Homeowners, HOAs, condo associations, and commercial property owners across the Colorado Front Range.

  • Goal

    Make it easy to request a case review or call the firm.

  • Constraint

    Explain six defect categories and establish the firm’s trial focus early.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Construction photo and case review form

    A job-site image and trial-focused headline sit beside a consultation form above the fold.

  2. 02

    Firm credentials

    A short section highlights past recoveries, the firm’s construction defect focus, its Front Range coverage, and the types of clients it represents.

  3. 03

    Six defect categories

    Cards cover roofs, windows, decks and balconies, structural issues, mechanical issues, and foundation or drainage concerns.

  4. 04

    Attorney profiles

    Trial attorney bios follow the defect categories so visitors can see who handles this work.

  5. 05

    Reviews and credentials

    Client reviews and firm credentials give visitors more context before the final CTA.

  6. 06

    Form or phone

    Visitors can request a case review through the closing form or call the firm.

03

Page architecture

  • Hero and formTrial focus and case review form.
  • Firm credentialsResults, practice focus, coverage, and client types.
  • Defect typesSix construction defect categories.
  • AttorneysTrial attorney profiles.
  • Reviews and credentialsClient reviews and information about the firm.
  • Closing CTACase review form and phone number.
04

Outcome

  • The first screen makes the firm’s trial focus clear.
  • Property owners can find the defect category that fits their concern and see who handles that work.
  • The form and phone number give visitors two ways to start a case review.
