About the project

How do I earn the bonus, and what do I do next? Those are the two questions I built the page around. The reward comes first, followed by the qualifying steps.

01

Challenge

Visitors can spot the dollar amount in seconds. The harder part is seeing exactly what qualifies. The page needed to make the requirements easy to scan, keep the application CTA easy to find, and sound like a local bank.

The brief
  • Audience

    Wisconsin households comparing checking accounts.

  • Goal

    Help eligible visitors start a checking account application.

  • Constraint

    Keep eligibility, qualifying activity, and offer terms visible.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    The offer up front

    Bank Five Nine’s red hero puts the $300 bonus next to a single Open Your Account CTA.

  2. 02

    Account benefits

    Four quick points cover the cash reward, free mobile banking, free debit card, and the bank’s community focus.

  3. 03

    How to earn the bonus

    Three steps cover regular deposits, debit card use, and online bill pay. The qualifying window appears beside them.

  4. 04

    Digital banking tools

    Four cards highlight online banking, mobile deposit, mobile wallet, and Zelle.

  5. 05

    Community banking

    A section on the bank’s local presence and FDIC membership appears before the closing CTA.

  6. 06

    Offer terms

    The terms and conditions sit directly below the final application prompt.

03

Page architecture

  • Hero$300 offer, short explanation, and application CTA.
  • Account benefitsFour reasons to consider the account.
  • How to earnThree qualifying steps and the timeframe.
  • Digital toolsOnline and mobile banking features.
  • Community bankingThe bank’s local presence and credentials.
  • Closing CTAApplication prompt and branch locator.
  • TermsEligibility and offer conditions.
04

Outcome

  • Visitors see the offer on the first screen and can review the requirements before applying.
  • The page explains how to qualify and gives visitors a way to find a branch.
  • The local banking details sit near the close, leaving the offer easy to spot up top.
