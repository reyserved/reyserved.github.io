About the project

Five Star makes custom polycarbonate windows for production runs. Buyers can see up front if their project fits, then check the specs and production process before asking for a quote.

01

Challenge

Paid search can bring in buyers looking for a single replacement window as well as teams sourcing at scale. The page had to state who the offer serves up front and cover coatings, compliance, complex shapes, and prototype timing without making buyers hunt for the form.

The brief
  • Audience

    OEMs, fleet operators, and large-scale manufacturers.

  • Goal

    Collect quote requests that include project type and order volume.

  • Constraint

    Make the production-order requirement clear and keep the form approachable.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Order fit and quote form

    The split hero states who the service is for and puts a seven-field quote form above the fold.

  2. 02

    Company credentials

    The page highlights 30+ years in business, ISO 9001, Made in the USA, and 200+ OEM partnerships.

  3. 03

    Coatings, prototypes, and specs

    Four cards cover Fusionite coatings, two-week prototypes, compliance, and complex formed shapes.

  4. 04

    In-house production

    The capabilities section shows how substrate enhancement, window fabrication, and finishing fit together.

  5. 05

    Applications

    A visual row covers fleet, safety, utility, heavy equipment, motorcycle, and racing applications.

  6. 06

    From consultation to delivery

    Five steps explain consultation, prototyping, testing, production, and delivery before the final quote CTA.

04

Outcome

  • The first screen makes the production-order requirement clear.
  • Buyers can review compliance and production details before requesting a quote.
  • The form gives sales the project type and order volume for the first conversation.
