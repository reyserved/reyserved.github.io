About the project

Moran Wealth Management had a guide for investors who wanted a plan for periods of market volatility. I built the page around what readers could use right away, from checklists and worksheets to questions they could bring to an advisor.

01

Challenge

When markets get volatile, it’s easy to get caught up in predictions. This page had to show what goes into a plan, from reviewing cash needs to deciding when to act. It also needed to explain what visitors would get for filling out the form and keep the download CTA easy to find within the constraints of financial marketing copy.

The brief
  • Audience

    Investors and families with significant assets who want a process they can return to when markets get volatile.

  • Goal

    Encourage guide downloads and give readers a starting point for a planning conversation.

  • Constraint

    Keep the copy focused on planning and make it clear that downloading the guide requires a form.

02

Execution

  1. 01

    Guide preview and form

    The hero puts the guide cover, a short description, and the download form above the fold.

  2. 02

    Who it’s for and what’s inside

    Two sections help visitors decide if the guide fits their situation and preview what they’ll get when they download it.

  3. 03

    Four-step planning framework

    The page walks through liquidity, risk, taxes and cash flow, and guardrails for making decisions under pressure.

  4. 04

    Checklists and decision filter

    The checklists show what to review before, during, and after a volatile period. A decision filter helps readers think through whether to act or wait.

  5. 05

    Prep for an advisor or CPA meeting

    The guide gives readers questions to ask and documents to gather before a planning conversation.

  6. 06

    Firm credentials and closing CTA

    The page introduces Moran’s credentials and ends with another chance to download the guide.

04

Outcome

  • Visitors can see the planning steps the guide covers, from reviewing cash needs to setting rules for decisions under pressure.
  • The page shows readers what they’ll get when they submit the download form.
  • The questions and document list give readers something concrete to bring to their next advisor or CPA meeting.
