Building and measuring an AI-answer visibility program

How Service Credit Union's product and membership pages earned citations in AI Overviews and LLM answers, and what the data still cannot prove.

01 · Objective

Make credit union content citable in AI answers

The goal was to turn Service Credit Union's product, membership, and educational pages into sources that AI Overviews and conversational engines would actually cite, while continuing to improve traditional click-through performance on high-intent pages.

That required two things at once: content that is easy for an answer engine to extract, and a measurement model that separates being cited from simply ranking.

02 · Insight

AI Overview was the strongest surface, but the program was behind target

In the July to August Semrush snapshot, AI Overview scored 39/100, well ahead of ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI Mode. Overall AI Visibility was 17/100 against a 44 target. Treating this as a finished GEO win would have been wrong.

The useful signal was at page level: citations moved from 888 to 892 and cited pages from 363 to 382 between the two rolling windows, with gains concentrated in definitional, membership, and product content.

03 · Strategy

Answer-first, question-led, source-backed content

The strategy was to make each priority page extractable before trying to make it more visible:

  • Define the query in the opening paragraph rather than easing into it.
  • Use question-led H2 and H3 sections that mirror real follow-up questions.
  • Keep fact-dense copy with named, authoritative sources.
  • Use FAQ blocks and appropriate structured data where they help extraction.
  • Measure page-level citations instead of relying on brand-level mentions.
04 · Execution

Page audits plus a two-source measurement model

I led the strategy and audited eight pages with at least good evidence, reviewing metadata, H1 to H4 structure, body copy, tables, FAQ content, JSON-LD, canonicals, robots, and llms.txt. Recommendations covered definitional intros, question headings, source citations, and FAQPage markup gaps.

Measurement paired Semrush Cited Pages with GSC Generative AI Features, so a page had to show citation evidence in one system and AI feature visibility in the other before it counted as a strong asset.

What this proves, and what it does not

What the evidence supports

  • Page-level citation breadth increased for several product and membership pages.
  • AI Overview was Service Credit Union's strongest AI surface in the measured windows.
  • The auto-loan hub showed both AI feature visibility and high citation counts in two independent sources.
  • A high-intent preapproval guide earned more clicks on nearly flat impressions.

What it does not support

  • Overall AI Visibility stayed at 17/100 against a 44 target; this is not a completed GEO win.
  • No clicks, calls, or leads are attributed to AI surfaces in the source data.
  • The vehicle-loans hub declined from 65 to 49 cited prompts, so gains were not uniform.
  • The two Semrush windows overlap by three days; changes are directional, not exact.
  • Page-level GSC AI-feature impression shares are withheld pending reconciliation of the source export.
